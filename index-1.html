Questions

Is UPWISE free?

Details on pricing will be shared before launch.

Can I trust the AI’s answers?

It is a helpful guide, not an authority. Check important facts with official sources, especially for health, legal and money decisions.

Is it only for Nigerian users?

No. UPWISE has a corner built for Nigerian youth, and the rest is useful anywhere.

Will it do my homework for me?

It explains concepts and helps you practise, but it is built to support learning, not assessment cheating.

What happens to my data?

You only see your own information, and you will be able to delete your account. We collect only what the product needs.
