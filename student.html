    Study techniques worth trying

    • Active recall: close the book and write what you remember, then check.
    • Spaced repetition: review the same topic over several days, not in one night.
    • Past questions: practise under timed conditions, then mark honestly.
    • Teach it back: explain a topic out loud in simple words.
    • Short blocks with real breaks beat long, tired sessions.
