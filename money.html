Money basics

  • Track your spending for a month before you try to cut anything.
  • A common rule of thumb is about half for needs, a third for wants and the rest for savings. Adjust it to your situation.
  • Build a small emergency buffer first, even a little each week.
  • Be careful with anything that promises guaranteed or very fast returns. That is a common sign of a scam.
  • Before borrowing, find out the full cost and the repayment terms.
