Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who we are

UPWISE is a learning, productivity and life-organisation platform. In this policy, “we” means UPWISE. You can reach us at upwiseai1@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored by our login provider in a protected form, and we cannot read them.
  • Profile details you choose to give: your name, country, time zone and interests.
  • What you add to the tools: goals, habits, exams, assignments, study blocks, income and expense records, and budgets.
  • Chats with the assistant, once the assistant is switched on: your messages and its replies, saved so you can find them later.
  • Technical data such as your IP address and browser type, which our hosting and database providers handle when you visit.

How we use it

We use your information to run your account, show your own records back to you, personalise suggestions using your country and interests, keep the service secure, and reply to your messages. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertising.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase provides our database and login system.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google to load them.
  • An AI provider receives the text of your messages only when you use the assistant, so it can write a reply.

On your device

Your browser stores your login session and a few settings, such as light or dark mode, your chosen currency and your saved checklist. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Security

Records in your account are protected by access rules so that only you can read or change them. Connections use HTTPS. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account exists. To have your account and records deleted, email us from the address on your account and we will delete them. You can also edit your profile at any time from your dashboard.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it, by emailing us.

Age

UPWISE is not meant for children under 13. If you are under the age of adulthood where you live, please use UPWISE with the permission of a parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Please check it from time to time.

Contact

Questions about privacy: upwiseai1@gmail.com.
